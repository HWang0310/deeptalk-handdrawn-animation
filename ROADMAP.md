# Roadmap

## V1 — deterministic grammar prototype

- SVG scene schema, timed element reveal, Chinese text, stroke/fill ordering.
- Seven local benchmark fixtures and rendered MP4 evidence.
- Machine QA for geometry, timing, clipping-risk, text, density, and final-frame coverage.

## V1.1 — controlled visual refinement (complete)

- Deterministic seeded coordinate wobble, line-width variation, and light duplicate-sketch pass on drawing primitives; Chinese text remains crisp.
- Fifteen original composable icon/annotation primitives, rendered as a separate local vocabulary sheet.
- Warning-only collision, spacing, focus, semantic-overlap, density, edge-safety, and final-hold assistance.
- Same seven V1 scenes re-rendered under V1.1 with varied reveal rhythm, local fill timing, a 700 ms final hold, QA JSON, and side-by-side final-frame evidence.

## V2 candidates — evaluate only after V1 evidence

- Optional hand-overlay experiment, isolated from the core renderer, only after a limited readability comparison.
- Remotion only if benchmark timelines prove SVG/FFmpeg orchestration is insufficient.
- Reviewed still-image background adapters; never delegate Chinese factual text to image generation.
